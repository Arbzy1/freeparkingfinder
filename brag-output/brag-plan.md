# freeparkingfinder launch video plan

**Angle:** A parking map that is more honest than you want it to be: a blank road is not free parking.
**Format:** 20 seconds, 1920x1080, 30fps, 120 BPM, original soundtrack, cuts on the beat.

| Time | Type | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | title | A blank road is not free parking. |
| 3-6s | title | Free parking finder |
| 6-12s | shot | Search a place. See the bays it can read. |
| 12-16s | list | Each in its own colour |
| 16-20s | outro | The sign at the kerb wins. |

## Source and stand-ins
- The map image is the README's own screenshot of Sutton. No people or vehicles are identified.

Public sites were loaded signed out with /api, /v1 and third-party requests blocked. No secrets or .env files were read.

## Check before posting
- This is Aden's project. Treat the video as a private draft and get Aden's approval before posting it.
- Coverage is limited to the councils in the README, so the video does not imply it covers everywhere.
